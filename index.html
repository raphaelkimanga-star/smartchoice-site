Core Competencies

What we deliver

Hands-on aviation maintenance is our core. Cyber threat intelligence is a complementary line built on Intelligence Community mission support.

J016 · 488190

Powertrain & Rotor Systems

Repair, inspection, and troubleshooting of helicopter powertrain, drive-train, and rotor systems. Non-destructive inspection (NDI), scheduled and unscheduled maintenance, and component removal and replacement.

J015 · 336411

Engine & Airframe Maintenance

Engine and airframe maintenance and inspections for fixed- and rotary-wing aircraft, transient aircraft services, and accurate maintenance records and technical documentation.

J017 · 811310

Ground Support Equipment

Maintenance, troubleshooting, and inspection of aviation ground support equipment in garrison and deployed environments.

Quality, Safety & Training

Cyber Threat Intelligence
