About SmartChoice

Veteran-led. Hangar-tested.

A service-disabled veteran-owned small business in Alexandria, Virginia.

SmartChoice Multi Services LLC is an SBA-certified Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) providing aviation maintenance, ground support equipment, and cyber threat intelligence services to federal, defense, and commercial customers.

Our Founder

Raphael Kimanga brings 18+ years of aviation maintenance and leadership. He began as an aircraft technician and safety officer with Kenya Airways and the Kenya Air Force, supported fixed- and rotary-wing operations at Kandahar Airfield, Afghanistan, and served as an aircraft powertrain repairer and shop supervisor in U.S. military aviation maintenance.

He has since applied the same discipline to cyber threat intelligence, supporting Defense Intelligence Agency and National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency missions. That combination of hands-on aircraft experience and intelligence analysis is rare in a small business.

How We Operate

Customers work directly with the founder. We take on work we know how to do, document it carefully, and treat safety and quality control as part of the job, not an afterthought.
